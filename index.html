The Curator

Ordinary Feats documents a personal collection of endurance events completed over the years. The purpose of the museum is not to celebrate extraordinary performances, but to preserve ordinary achievements that required extraordinary commitment. Every exhibit represents a finished challenge. Nothing more. Nothing less.
